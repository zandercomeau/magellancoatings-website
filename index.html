We use the personal information collected through our contact form solely to:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain your submitted contact information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or to comply with legal obligations. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Your Rights

  • The right to withdraw consent for processing your data at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.
